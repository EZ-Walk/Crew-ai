**Training Program Package**

**Training Materials**
We will develop powerpoint presentations, videos tutorials and written manuals for each significant area of the policy. The materials will be designed in simple language with clear instructions, diagrams, and screenshots.

1. **Presentation on Objective and Scope of Remote Work Policy.**
2. **Video tutorials and manuals on use of Slack, Teams, Asana, and Trello.**
3. **Guidelines for maintaining security using VPNs, secure cloud storage, and password managers.**
4. **Instructions on effective virtual donor and beneficiary communications.**
5. **BYOD Policy Adoption Guide and its financial advantages.**
6. **Guide on BambooHR or Zenefits and the impact on HR and finance.**

**Assessment Tools**
To ensure effective learning, digital quizzes and practical tasks will be used to evaluate understanding and application of the policy.

1. **Quiz on policy objectives and scope.**
2. **Technical tasks on Slack, Teams, Asana, Trello for practical assessment.**
3. **Scenario-based assessments on maintaining security protocols and BYOD policy.**
4. **Simulation of virtual communication with donors.**
5. **Tasks on BambooHR or Zenefits to evaluate understanding of HR and financial management.**

**Implementation Schedule**
The training program will be implemented in phases over an 8-week period. 

1. **Week 1: Policy objectives and overview**
2. **Week 2-3: Tools training (Slack, Teams, Asana, Trello, VPNs, storage, password managers)**
3. **Week 4: Virtual communications workshop**
4. **Week 5: BYOD Policy and security protocols training**
5. **Week 6-7: Bamboo HR or Zenefits training**
6. **Week 8: Final assessments**

**Success Metrics**
To evaluate the success of the training program, we will track the following:

1. **Quiz scores: Aim for average 85% pass rate across all quizzes.**
2. **Task evaluations: 90% of staff able to complete practical tasks effectively.**
3. **Feedback surveys: Aim for 75% positive responses in post-training surveys.**
4. **Policy adherence: Monitor incidences of policy violations, aim for less than 5% within first two months of implementation.**
5. **Operation efficiency: Aim for less than 10% decrease in operational efficiency or service delivery during transition period.** 

This comprehensive training program will ensure staff and volunteers at all levels are equipped to transition effectively to a remote working environment while maintaining the integrity and efficiency of the non-profit.
